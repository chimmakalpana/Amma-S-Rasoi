Heritage & Care

Born in a family kitchen with heirloom recipes.

Amma's Rasoi was founded on one simple belief: food made with pure love brings comfort, health, and joy to the soul.

Tired of restaurant takeaways loaded with synthetic colors, palm oil, and day-old frozen curries, we started cooking wholesome, home-style meals for friends and neighbours in Hyderabad.

Every single dawn begins at 5:00 AM with stone-grinding fresh masalas, soaking wholesome grains, and slow-simmering biryani in traditional clay handis. We never compromise on ingredients, hygiene, or authentic warmth.

"If we wouldn't serve it to our own children at our kitchen table, it will never leave our kitchen for yours."

— Amma & Family, Founders
